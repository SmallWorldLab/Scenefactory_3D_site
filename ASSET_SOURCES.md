## Vehicle demos

- `assets/videos/vehicles/{brake,grade,hills,hauler,closure,courier,cargo_stop,cargo_fishhook}.mp4` and `assets/images/vehicles/*.jpg`
  (posters) are generated by `tools/vehicle_demos/` from the astraexplore branch `vehicle-family`
  (rev recorded in each log's `code_rev`). Physics: `astra_drive.vehicle_family.gpu.FamilyBatch` on the GPU at 240 Hz
  (each log's `device`), which computes the released GPU kernel's formulas for the Coupe and Crossover.
  Rendering: three.js in headless Chrome, 1280×720 at 30 fps, H.264 CRF 24. Bodies are drawn over the
  collision boxes. These are hand-authored demonstrations, not benchmark episodes, and the page says so.
